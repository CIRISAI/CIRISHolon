## 0. The four tests

| test | a "yes" means | one of these is enough for "no" |
|---|---|---|
| **FORCED** | the number follows from the closure, section or price machinery plus inputs already banked | any free choice: which relaxation time, which criterion, which window, which coefficient, a bar taken from the read |
| **BLIND** | not in print as a number; not computed on either record; not bracketed by a pilot or a reviewer's grid | the designer has seen the outcome, or two points either side of it |
| **REACHABLE** | a carrier and an instrument exist or are small to build; the run fits the machine; a referee can check the instrument | a long run, a missing engine path, no referee |
| **COULD COME OUT OTHERWISE** | a named outcome would refute it, and the programme does not know today which way it goes | every outcome can be absorbed, or the answer is a theorem |

"About" says what the prediction is a statement about: OUR MODEL (a model or instrument this
programme built), A PUBLISHED MODEL (someone else's equations), or THE WORLD (a measurement).

## 2. Each candidate

**1. The reader rule on unintegrated rows (T5, extended).** *What exists.* The sibling froze
and ran T5 on 2026-11-16 (`scratchpad/lockdown/t5_baryo/RESULTS.md`; being vendored here on
branch `baryo-vendor-2026-11-23`). Its rule: a pure-dephasing reader never writes
populations, and it leaks the washout-protected direction at the golden-rule rate
`2κ cos²θ sin²θ`, independent of the washout `W`. On the literature's own density-matrix
equation (Blanchet, Di Bari, Jones and Marzola, arXiv 1112.4528) that rule, frozen before
the arms existed, predicted the first-order slope to seven digits, the crossover midpoint
within `0.81–1.21 ×` on thirteen rows of which eleven were unseen, and the sign and size of
its fall with `K`. Two arms were KILLED: the interpolation is not monotone (an overshoot up
to `1.5 ×` at `K = 1000`), and one weak-washout row broke the `κ90` edge. *Free choices.*
None in the leak rate. In the crossover: the single-exponential closure, which T5's own
residual shows is not exact (the ratio drifts from `1.0` to `0.81` with `K`); a band of
`0.5–2.0` was wide enough to hide that. A forward stake has to predict the DRIFT, or narrow
the band to what the closure's own error term allows. *The forward rows.* (a) `K` and `p`
outside T5's grid, and the weak-washout corner where it was killed: does the rule say in
advance where it fails (`κ ≪ W` over the leak window stops holding)? (b) The overshoot's
size as a function of `K`, from `λ_min` staying under the flavoured slow rate — T5 derived
the mechanism, staked monotone anyway, and found the overshoot post hoc; a derived size
written before a finer grid is a real stake. (c) Three flavours: the same rule with the muon
Yukawa in place of the tau's gives a second crossover; Moffat, Pascoli, Petcov, Schulz and
Turner (arXiv 1804.05066) solved the three-flavour density-matrix equations across exactly
that transition. *Value exists in print, not read.* That paper is the outside referee: the
rule's call for where their curves leave the two- and three-flavour limits, written before
their figure is opened. (d) Thermal-mass oscillations restored: Beneke et al. (arXiv
1007.4783) show a bump of about 4 % where T5's toy shows up to 50 %; that figure WAS viewed
by the sibling, so the bump's size is not blind; its dependence on `K` may be. *Prior art.*
The Zeno statement and the protected direction are Blanchet, Di Bari and Raffelt's
(hep-ph/0611337) and BDJM's; T5 grades itself "convergent-credited" and says the rule
language mostly renames. What is the rule's own: the `W`-independent leak law and the
overshoot's size. *Referee.* T5's five gauges; the unflavoured and fully flavoured Boltzmann
limits; the published three-flavour curves. *Kill.* A forward row outside the band the
closure's error term gives. *First task.* BARYO-REPRO-0: the independent implementation
reproduces T5, passes and kills alike. Until then nothing new may be staked on it.

**2. Equilibrium predicts the drives.** By fluctuation and dissipation, the equilibrium

of the fit window at a signal-to-noise of 6 to 9, in which case one law holds at both
drives. But the designer has seen both inputs. *Prior art.* Textbook (Kubo). It tests the
record, not nature. *Referee.* The planted threefold change of damping and the planted same
law, on the carrier's own noise. *Kill.* The equilibrium law misses the 50 m/s arms beyond
their noise: then the instrument or the arms are wrong, and RESPONSE-1's verdicts are in
question from the other side. *First task.* The redesigned rung 0 of
`SEAM_NS0_PREREG_DRAFT.md`, reviewed again. *What it earns.* Up to three corrections to
RESPONSE-1, or its confirmation. Not the bar.

## 4. Where prediction can begin

**First: put the reader rule on the engine and stake it forward (candidate 1).** It is the
only machinery on either record that has made advance calls that passed AND kills that
fired, on equations it did not write. The reproduction is already in work. The forward
stake with an outside referee is the three-flavour crossover against Moffat et al.'s
published curves, unopened. Against the bar of 2026-11-13: it would be a forced derivation
of a nontrivial piece of known THEORY — where a computed transition sits — not of a measured
fact; no experiment has seen the flavour crossover. Against the purpose sentence: it says
which view of the lepton density matrix is real (closed) at which temperature, with a price,
which is the kind of statement this programme exists to make. Honest ceiling: T5 graded
itself as mostly renaming; what is new is a leak law and an overshoot, in a toy.

**Second, at once and cheaply: the two record tests (candidates 2 and 3).** They predict
nothing about nature. They decide whether three sentences of RESPONSE-1 stand, on data
already on disk, and candidate 3's number is already committed. A programme that is going
to predict has to know its own reader does not turn an oscillation into a decay at low
signal-to-noise.

**Third: the frozen generator against an unread year (candidate 4).** The only one about the
world that passes all four tests. It is small and it is not physics, and that is the point
to say plainly: the place this record can today make a forward call about the world and be
wrong is the generated scene, because that is where it has a frozen object, a public
reference that keeps arriving, and budgets written before the read.

What does not qualify, and should not be dressed as if it did: rung IV until a number is
derived; the QVM's pick; anything cosmological before DR3; the Jarlskog reading.

**The gap this leaves.** Nothing on either record is forced, blind, reachable, able to fail
AND about a measured fact of physics. The nearest routes: (i) a derived coefficient for the
sibling's `t7` (`n` selected by something), which would turn a wager into a prediction of
`|V_cb|`; (ii) a derivation for rung IV; (iii) the reader rule applied to a system with a
laboratory measurement of a decoherence-set crossover — which neither record has looked for,
and which is the search this survey would do next.
