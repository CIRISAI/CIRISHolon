# The conformance battery

These adjudicators are the acceptance tests a tier must pass, transplanted
from the CIRISOntology campaign record where each band was frozen by
pre-registration, gauged two-sided on planted truth (every arm demonstrated
able to fail before any real run), and exercised across multiple geometries.
Paths inside the scripts still reflect their origin; adapting them to this
repository's layout is part of milestone one and MUST NOT change a band.

## Why the tests are interventional

Observational closure is not identifiable on a deterministic substrate: a
single orbit cannot distinguish a Closed view from an unclosed one (the
upstream `interventional_iff_closed` and its orbit-non-identifiability
companion). Certification therefore always runs TWIN sessions — probed vs
sham, or macro-matched micro-different — and reads divergences, never
absolute trajectories.

## The battery

- `analyze_idjoin.py` — twin-session backbone: sham exactness, pre-probe
  exactness, per-holon light-cone ordering, and the rise-epoch growth budget
  (K ≤ 1.05; plateau-including windows are refused — a saturating expander
  reads 1.0 on them).
- `tier_battery.py` + `analyze_tier.py` — tier-view certification:
  construction premise on the PRE-step frame, the Aggregation budget, the
  conditioning level law (early-time scope), and the organization/protection
  stakes on growth residuals with a 64-view fair control (symmetric-moment
  charts, every control view exactly as blind to the perturbation as the
  declared ones).
- `nabla_adjudicate.py` — chart-transport law: mass-weighted plaquette
  holonomy against the derived operator (I+Δx/4)(I+Δy/4)−I, with the
  uniform-weight null as pipeline premise (must reproduce the derivation to
  ≤ 1e-12).
- `onset_analyzer.py` — threshold-relative onsets (1% of max) and the
  in-job premise check pattern; bands anchored to same-run calibration,
  never to a prior epoch's constants.

## The standing rules

Every gate ships with planted-mutation evidence that it can fire; every
band names its units and its frame-zero; every conserved quantity gets its
own gate; a reading that cannot vary under the data's generative structure
is VOID, not PASS.
