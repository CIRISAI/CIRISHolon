*The prereg audit refuses a freeze whose text contacts a registered misfit's
keywords without citing it. That is the mechanism that turns "we knew this"
into "we applied this": the registry is grep-armed, not advisory.*

| **M-IDLE-CALIBRATED-TIMEOUT** | **a sensor not calibrated in the REGIME IT RUNS IN fails silently and optimistically**, and that is what makes it dangerous rather than merely wrong — it does not throw, it blesses. Two faces, found independently by two lanes and one law: the WRONG ENVIRONMENT (a constant measured idle and applied loaded) and the WRONG DURATION (a probe shorter than the timescale of the thing it measures). RESOURCE-1's reaper is the first: polling a real 72-node generation at loadavg 40+ it measured its holders' true step at 402–702 polls (2–3.5 s) per H3 node against the ~8 ms an idle box gives — a factor of ~400 — and a flat grace sized from idle timings convicted 1115 LIVE workers (0 after the grace was made self-calibrating). bigqvm's quiet-window gate is the second: its 55 ms calibration job read 0.055 s against a 0.052 s record AT LOADAVG 46 — 1.06×, inside any sane gate — and so certified a saturated box as quiet, because a job shorter than the scheduler's contention timescale gets an idle core no matter what; only a 1.3 s job separated the regimes (`conformance/bigqvm/run_when_quiet.sh`, whose header keeps the rejected d=45 gate as the transferable failure). Note the symmetry of the harm: an idle-calibrated timeout REAPS HEALTHY WORK, a short probe BLESSES A LOADED BOX — opposite errors, same silent optimism. The fix is structural in both cases and never a bigger constant: learn the constant from the holder's own completed work in the environment where it runs, size the probe above the contention timescale, and refuse to judge a holder still in WARMUP that has not completed a step. Temporal precedent: the hardware campaign's calibrations-are-rented lesson (a band frozen from a prior epoch decayed 88×). NEIGHBOUR, distinct and bigqvm's to name if they want an id: a SINGLE-SAMPLE sensor cannot see a REGIME CHANGE — loadavg read only at a sweep's start misses started-quiet-finished-loaded — which shares the silent-optimistic failure but has a different fix (sample both ends and refuse the label when they disagree, as their sweep now does) rather than a different calibration | RESOURCE-1 live reaper + bigqvm quiet-window gate, 2026-08-30 |
| **M-PROBE-THE-RESOURCE** | a health probe that interrogates the HOLDER ("is the process scheduling") instead of the RESOURCE ("can I actually write") passes exactly while every real operation fails: during the 2026-08-30 disk-full window every writer on the box was scheduling, burning CPU, and unable to write a byte — healthy by any process probe, dead by the only probe that matters. | **M-PLACEMENT-LOTTERY** | on a HETEROGENEOUS core (this box is an i9-13900HX: P-cores 0–15, E-cores 16–31, scaling 57%) a wall-clock comparison is confounded by where the scheduler put each arm, and the MINIMUM over repetitions does not remove it — it recovers the luckiest PLACEMENT, not an uncontended floor. Measured 2026-08-30 with both arms pinned to the SAME core: the d=101 head-to-head verdict FLIPS with placement (0.822 unpinned "we lead", 1.201 on a P-core "they lead by 20%", 0.989 on an E-core), and one banked row was RETRACTED on it; a d=141 "record" of 8.264 s (min of 5, unpinned) is beaten by 6.274 s pinned at loadavg 44.6, so the minimum was never a floor. The estimator argument that fails here is exactly "interference can only ADD time, so the minimum is robust" — true of contention, false of placement, and the two are not distinguishable from the wall clock. Pinning also cut repetition spread from 15–29% to 1.1–1.7% and HALVED both arms at d=221, so unpinned runs lost more to migration than to the neighbours. Remedy: PIN both arms to the same core (`taskset`, or `sched_setaffinity` on the harness so children inherit) and report BOTH core types with the ADVERSARIAL one as the headline; gate on CPU time (`/proc/self/stat` 14+15) not wall, since descheduling does not inflate it. Distinct from [[M-IDLE-CALIBRATED-TIMEOUT]]: that is a sensor mis-calibrated for its regime, this is a comparison whose confounding axis was never controlled — a quiet window fixes contention and does NOTHING about heterogeneity.
