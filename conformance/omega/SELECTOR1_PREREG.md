## The pass criteria (operational, per the converged exchange — a
candidate dynamics T passes only if it contains a subsystem P such that)

1. P internally generates an intervention U (a knob in Break.lean's
   sense, produced by T's own restriction to P — not applied by hand);
2. U reduces or bounds another quotient's closure defect or rent;
3. a conserved resource ledger pays U's cost (the ledger's conservation
   verified exactly);
4. ablating P destroys the maintained closure (the ablation gate);
5. T maintains or reconstructs P (the builder gate);
6. throughput and intervention are nonzero — excluding the fixed point
   BY CRITERION, not by hand;
7. the selector uses ONLY Ω observables (views, rents, defects, ledgers)
   and imports no label derived from T's desired identity.
