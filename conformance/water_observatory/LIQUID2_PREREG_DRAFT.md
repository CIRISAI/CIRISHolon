`6 × 6 × 6` box stands, and this is the measurement behind it.

## 4. The gap this crosses, named (M-UNTESTED-GAP)

**Two things this campaign does NOT do, stated before it runs so that nothing later reads as
if it did.**

**The density is IMPOSED.** `0.997 g/cm³` is CHOSEN, declared as half the state point, and it
fixes the cell edge exactly. Nothing in this campaign predicts water's density, tests it, or
could: the box is built at that density and held there by a periodic cell that never moves.
A law that got the density wrong would look identical here. Predicting a density needs a
barostat and a free volume, and the engine has an NPT integrator this campaign does not use.

**The basis is minimal and the law is exact for its Hamiltonian, not for water.** Every
coefficient in `ct3/wall_ct3.json` came from exact solves in a minimal (STO-3G) basis. At that
basis attunement — channel 3, pair dispersion — reads an exact ZERO, and no dispersion
coefficient is transferred in from anywhere: `c6` is `0.000000` in the record by FIELD-6's
rule, and LIQUID-1's `C6_45` screen, which put a literature value there, is a SCREEN and
enters nothing. So the law served here is the exact law of a minimal-basis Hamiltonian, and
the gap between that Hamiltonian and water is not this campaign's to close. Every reading
below is a reading of what that Hamiltonian does at this state point. Where it misses water,
the two named candidates are the basis (I-5, §4's second branch, not available) and the
channels the basis cannot carry — and neither is a defect of the liquid.

A law every coefficient of which was fit on TWO molecules, asked about 128 in contact; three
seeds, one box size counted plus one size check, one temperature, once. And one thing this
campaign inherits rather than fixes: the whole of §2a, which says the ledger of the arm it is
about to run is being pushed three orders harder than the same box on the same step under the
law without channel 6. CT-3's own two named gaps travel with it and are
restated here rather than left in its results file: the map has NO knot between its two
azimuth sheets at high tilt, which is where its measured 8 % lives, and it has no node with a
pair donating in BOTH directions at once — which a liquid has on nearly every frame and which
the serving rule cannot represent. The table is served anyway, and this is the freeze that
says so in advance.

**The pre-committed branches, each with its kill.**

1. **Accommodation at the box — the FIRST branch, and it fires if R2 reads (b).** Channel 2 is
   the one FIELD-1's results named and no arm has run: the mutual embedding's fixed point
   (`holon_chem::seam`, "the fixed point the library already computes and the force law does
   not") taken over the 128 units, so each unit's charge is the one the other 127 leave it
   with, rather than the pin's fixed `0.231380` on each hydrogen and `-0.462761` on the oxygen. LIQUID-1's `Q125` screen turned that knob
   crudely (charges × 1.25, the enhancement every fixed-charge water model needs) and moved
   the bond count by 11 %; the branch replaces the knob with the computation. Its kill, staked
   now: the fixed point over 128 units does not converge inside the price the branch is given,
   or it converges and the bond count does not enter `[1.5, 2.0]` — in which case
   accommodation is not the missing connectivity either and the next channel is named by
   elimination rather than by preference.
2. **Dispersion from the basis lane — NOT AVAILABLE, and therefore a FENCE and not a branch.**
   Attunement (channel 3) reads an exact zero at this basis, and the dispersion coefficient
   this law would need does not exist to be served: it requires I-5, one basis with virtual p
   on hydrogen and a diffuse function on oxygen, which has not been built. LIQUID-1's `C6_45`
   screen used a LITERATURE `C₆` and is a screen — a declared knob, never a run, and nothing it
   wrote enters a gate or a claim. Owner: the basis lane (I-5, GANTT2 "Molecular"). Exit: the
   first `C₆` that lane harvests from its own solves. Until then this branch cannot be run and
   this freeze does not pretend it could.

## 5. Plants

Three, each pre-checked on BOTH conditions before any counted frame and with no measurement at
all: the carrier is nonzero in the sector the plant acts on, and the observable can move as
far as the stake by the freeze's own arithmetic (the second is FLUID-1's correction, where two
plants were unreachable by the freeze's own numbers before a single step ran).

- **(i) The radial distribution without the minimum image.** `rdf_oo` on raw differences (a
  cell of `1e6` bohr, so no reduction fires, renormalised from that cell's density — a scalar
  rescale, so the plant is the histogram's change and nothing else): the first peak's height
  must change by more than `20 %`. Carrier: the fraction of O–O pairs inside the first shell
  (`3.2` Å, converted through the lens's own bohr to `6.047124`) whose minimum-image vector
  crosses a face, asserted nonzero in the sector the plant acts on, with a floor of `5 %`.
  Analytic reach: removing the minimum image moves EXACTLY the crossing pairs out of the shell,
  so the peak's fractional change is at most that crossing fraction — the carrier IS the reach,
  and the pre-check is therefore the statement `carrier > 0.20`.
- **(ii) The hydrogen-bond count without the minimum image.** `hbonds` (the open-box lens) on
  the wrapped positions: the count per molecule must fall by more than `20 %`. Same carrier,
  same sector, same reach and the same arithmetic.
- **(iii) The spanning cluster with the wrap counts zeroed.** The same H-bond graph with every
  edge's integer displacement set to `[0, 0, 0]`: the spanning fraction must move by more than
  `0.5`. Carrier: the fraction of H-bond edges whose wrap count is not zero, asserted nonzero
  in the sector the plant acts on, floor `5 %`. Analytic reach: a winding is exhibited ONLY by
  an edge with a nonzero wrap count, so zeroing every wrap count makes a winding
  unconstructible and the spanning fraction can move by its whole range, `1.0`.

**Where the carrier is measured, and why that is a finding.** On the SETTLED box, over 100
frames after the settling and before any counted frame — not at the door. At the door the
carrier is exactly `NaN`: the bcc start puts its nearest oxygens `6.41` bohr apart and the
first shell is `6.047`, so the sector plants (i) and (ii) act on contains no pair at all and
`Plant::pre_check` reads `CarrierEmpty` on a box that will carry a quarter of its first-shell
pairs across a face within the settling. That is M-EMPTY-SECTOR firing on this freeze's own
instrument, caught before any arm, and it is recorded here rather than in a postmortem. The
counted arm measures the same carrier again over its own readout frames.

## 6. Discipline

**The step sweep is a SCREEN and nothing it writes enters a gate or a claim.** Ten labelled
arms (`screen --step <x> --law ct3|ct3-noct|ct2`), every record carrying `"dry": true` and its
label through `RecordWriter::screen`, equal in settling and in counted frames so that the knob
is the only thing that moved, each reporting its drift peak, its thermostat column, their
ratio against L1's bar fraction, its per-pass cost, its mean temperature, its momentum
residual, its unit count and the step it actually held to the bit. Its ONE use is to choose
what this freeze pre-commits, which is what the screen law allows — and on this campaign it
did more work than that, because it is what found section 2a. The first four arms are kept
under `screen/first_pass_dt_reference_units/` with a README: their measurements stand, and
what was wrong was the unit their knob was reported in (`dt_reference` rather than the step in
force), which the README converts.

**What changed in the engine, and what did not.** The campaign itself is one example and
one DEV-dependency (`holon-campaign` added to `holon-render`'s `[dev-dependencies]`, zero
runtime dependencies, std only, never linked into the library or the browser artifact — the
same isolation profile and the same one-way direction as `holon-lens` beside it). `field.rs`
and `liquid1.rs` are untouched. FOUR INSTRUMENTS DID CHANGE, each of them an answer to a
source claim of a review, and each of them ADDITIVE — the existing callers of record keep the
existing behaviour and every existing test of `holon-render`, `holon-lens`, `holon-campaign`
and `holon-closure` passes unchanged:
- `holon-render/src/seam.rs` gains `CtServe::{Argmin, Blend}`, `CtTable::{set_blend,
  set_argmin, serve_mode, beta}` and `CtTable::serve_blend`, with `sim.rs::accumulate_seam`
  branching on the mode (the smooth serving rule, §2a). **`Argmin` is the DEFAULT and the
  selector lives on the TABLE**, so every record written before the rule existed reads the
  argmin bit for bit — which is also exactly why the campaign had to be made to SELECT the
  blend rather than inherit it (gate CONFIG, M-VALIDATED-NOT-WIRED).
- `holon-lens/src/lens.rs` gains `diffusion_periodic` (the boundary-aware lens, R3 above).
  **`diffusion` itself is byte-unchanged** and keeps its callers of record, including the
  refusal LIQUID-1 published; the new function is a second lens beside it and not an edit
  to the first.
- `holon-render/src/thermostat.rs` is new and `sim.rs::apply_thermostat` branches on a new
  `thermostat_kind` field whose default is `Berendsen` (gate T above). The default path
  draws no random number at all and is bit-for-bit what it was, which is asserted rather
  than asserted-to-be-obvious: `thermostat_draws() == 0` after a Berendsen run, and two
  Berendsen runs of one scene are compared bit for bit.
- `holon-campaign/src/uncertainty.rs` is new: the statistical inefficiency and Chodera's
  equilibration rule (gates E and N above). It holds no constant and no band, like the rest
  of the harness.

Runner `holon-render/examples/liquid2.rs`, written on `holon-campaign` and `holon-closure`;
`liquid1.rs` is untouched and is the reference. Phases `screen`, `size`, `cost`, `gate`
(`gate --demo-unswitched` for gate CONFIG's own demonstration, which writes nothing),
`pilot --pilot i [--dir D]` / `pilot --freeze [--dir D]`, `run --seed k [--thermostat
berendsen]`, `read`. **Three of those flags exist so that a re-measurement never lands on a
banked record**: `cost --tag`/`--selection` (the `1×` re-price beside the `8×` bank, and the
`8×` control re-run in the same mix), `pilot --dir` (a pilot set taken at one step never

screen keeps the OLD configuration — argmin, Berendsen, its own `--step` — so every screen
record already in this tree re-runs bit for bit. **The pilot phase is where gate
E's floor comes from and it is the one phase whose output is never a readout of this
campaign**: it writes only into `liquid2/pilot/`, its records say `"is_a_pilot": true` and
`"no_readout_of_this_campaign_is_taken_from_it": true`, its seeds are disjoint from the

aggregates every seed that finished, refuses to count anything a screen wrote or any arm that
