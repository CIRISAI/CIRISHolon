## 0. WHY THIS EXISTS

`Sim::derive_pair_cutoff` (`sim.rs:1584`) **floors `r_in` at the table's own last knot**: it
starts at `r_max` and only ever walks outward, so the first branch returns `r_in = r_max`
whenever the curve is already under the declared budget there. The O–O curve's `r_max` is
**20.0** bohr and `|u(20.0)| = 6.641237e-7` Ha, already under carrier-v2's `PAIR_FLOOR = 1e-6`
— so `r_in = 20.0` and `r_cut = 22.0` **at every budget a caller can declare**. `list_cutoff`
inherits it, and `CellList::rebuild` needs 3 cells per axis, so the O(N) route cannot engage
below an extent of `3 × 22.0 = 66.0` bohr.

At carrier-v2's `RHO_ATOMS = 0.014860` atoms/bohr³ that is

```
N = RHO * (3 * r_cut)^3 = 0.014860 * 27 * (r_max + 2)^3 = 0.40122 * (r_max + 2)^3
  = 0.40122 * 22.0^3 = 4272
```

**atoms before the route can engage at all** — against carrier-v2's N = 800 successor bar.
The programme is stuck behind a radius that no budget can move, because the budget is not
what sets it.

### 0.1 The design target, derived rather than chosen

Solving the same expression for the bar:

```
0.40122 * (r_max + 2)^3 <= 800   =>   r_max <= 10.59 bohr
```

H–H and O–H already stop at **10.2400**. So the staked cut is **O–O to 10.2400**, which
invents no new number, makes the near bank's support uniform, and gives a derived threshold
of `0.40122 × 12.24³ = 736`. **Derived, not measured** — §6's G-ROUTE measures it, and the
measured number is the deliverable.

### 0.2 The pairing is not optional, and this freeze does not get to assume it works

A shorter near table **without** the far sector recreates exactly the discard B1b measured
and B2 paid for. The far sector must carry `(10.24, 20.0]` — and **that is a band its tail
model has never been validated on.** `B2_RESULTS.md` §2 fitted the O–O exponent over the last
10% of the curve's knots, `p_fit = 5.0049` with residual 3.7e-5, which is a statement about
~18–20 bohr. Extrapolating it down a full decade is precisely M-UNTESTED-GAP, and it is the
first thing this freeze measures.

## 1. THE ARTIFACT

The near table is a **NEW artifact with its own identity**, never an edit of the banked
96-knot O–O curve, whose record — including its `IterationCap` exit at 5000 iterations and
worst residual 4.809e-6 — stays exactly as it is. Its form is being settled with
`saturation2-water`, who owns the curve; **this freeze stakes the gates, and the two admissible
forms are gated differently:**

* **PREFIX form** — same generator, same grid, truncated at 10.2400, so every retained knot is
  bit-identical to one in the banked curve. Then G-EXACT below is a **bit-comparison**.
* **RESAMPLED form** — same generator, 96 knots redistributed over the shorter range. Then
  G-EXACT is a **budgeted** comparison against the banked curve's interpolant, and the freeze
  says so rather than quietly weakening a bit-gate into a tolerance.

Whichever lands, the near table declares which form it is, and the instrument REFUSES to
score G-EXACT under the bit-comparison unless the artifact declares itself a prefix.

## 2. THE GATES

- **G-SPLIT — THE ADMISSIBLE HANDOVER RADIUS, and the gate that can refute this design.** For candidate `R_s` on the staked ladder `[10.24, 11.0, 12.0, 13.0, 14.0, 15.0, 16.0, 18.0, 20.0]` bohr, the tail model is rebuilt at that `R_s` and `max |u_far(r) − u_table(r)|` is measured over `(R_s, 20.0]` at 200 sampled radii. A radius is ADMISSIBLE when that worst gap is below `1.0e-7` Ha — one tenth of carrier-v2's own `PAIR_FLOOR`, so a pair the far sector carries wrongly is worth less than a tenth of the error the near sector is already allowed to drop. The gap is printed for EVERY candidate, admissible or not. witness: `DependsWithinUpTo`
- **G-EXACT — THE ZERO-DISCARD REFEREE.** On B1b's own admitted frames, under its own manifest refusal, the re-split configuration's total pair interaction is compared against the FULL-TABLE complete sum, per frame. Three regions, gated separately because they fail differently: pairs at `r <= R_s` must agree EXACTLY (bit-identical under the prefix form, else within 1.0e-12 relative); pairs in `(R_s, R_f]` within the G-SPLIT budget of 1.0e-7 Ha per pair; pairs past `R_f` counted and reported as the residual discard, gated at `0.10·D_s` exactly as B1b gated its own. witness: `DependsWithinUpTo`
- **G-ROUTE — THE DELIVERABLE.** The smallest N at which `Sim::route()` reports `Cells`, MEASURED by bisection on a 3D liquid-density scene at the banked radius and at the re-split radius, with the cell arithmetic (`cells_per_axis`, extent, cutoff) printed at every probe. A ROUTE reading and not a timing, so placement cannot move it. Both numbers are reported beside their derived expectations of 4272 and 736; a measured value differing from its derivation by more than 20% is REPORTED as a finding about the derivation, not silently accepted. witness: `none (a measured property of the engine's own decomposition; M-VOLUME-SCALE is its warrant)`
- **G-LAWS — THE THREE LAWS IN BOTH CONFIGURATIONS.** Energy, momentum and angular momentum, each staked on the quantity its own law constrains, run at the banked radius and at the re-split radius over at least 20000 steps. A re-split that buys the route by breaking a conservation law has bought nothing. witness: `none (conservation gates on this engine's own ledgers; M-NULL-MISSTAKE is its warrant)`
- **G-LEGAL — THE REFUSALS STILL FIRE.** The re-split lowers `legality_radius`, so boxes that were illegal become legal. Every refusal is re-exercised at the new radius: sub-support (R3), the periodic floor, `scale_box`'s door. A refusal that stops firing because the radius moved is a refusal that was measuring the radius. witness: `none (a refusal-observability gate; the cryo finding is its warrant)`
- **G-WORK — WORK COUNT.** Printed for every arm: candidate radii probed, sampled gap radii, frames scored, N values bisected, steps integrated. A gap measured over an empty band and a threshold measured on a scene that never spans 3 cells are the same failure. witness: `none (an anti-vacuity assertion; M-VACUOUS-SUCCESS is its warrant)`
- **G-SOLVE — THE NEAR TABLE DID WORK.** The near table prints `route`, `exit`, `n_det`, `n_basis`, `solver_budget` and `worst_residual`, and the banked curve's own `IterationCap` / 5000 / 4.809e-6 travel beside every constant derived from either. witness: `none (a work-evidence and disclosure gate; M-CHEAPER-THAN-ITS-PRICE and M-EXIT-DISCRIMINATOR are its warrants)`

## 3. PLANTS

**P-GAP — against G-SPLIT.** Perturb the fitted exponent by 1%. *Carrier:* the change in the
worst gap over the band. *Sector:* the tail model. Nonzero because the gap depends on `p`
through `r^(−p)` over a decade, so a 1% exponent error is a factor of `10^0.01·p ≈ 1.12` at
the band's far end.

**P-BAND — against G-EXACT.** Drop the far sector's contribution over `(R_s, r_max]`
entirely. *Carrier:* the omitted band's energy. *Sector:* the far pair sector. Nonzero because
B1b measured that band at up to 1.150526e-5 Ha, which is the number this whole design exists
to keep.

**P-ROUTE — against G-ROUTE.** Force the complete route (`RoutePolicy::Complete`).
*Carrier:* the measured threshold. *Sector:* the decomposition. The bisection must fail to
find any N, rather than reporting a large one — a threshold search that returns a number on a
scene that can never take the route is measuring its own upper bound.

**P-LAWS — against G-LAWS.** The non-central far force from B2's P3, unchanged. *Carrier:*
`|L − L₀|`. *Sector:* angular. It must fire the angular gate while the momentum gate stays
green, exactly as it does in B2's battery — if it stops doing that under the re-split, the
re-split changed something the freeze did not intend.

**P-LEGAL — against G-LEGAL.** A periodic box between the two legality floors: legal under
the re-split radius, illegal under the banked one. *Carrier:* the differing verdict.
*Sector:* the legality radius. This is the plant that proves the two radii are actually
different where it matters, and it is the one that would catch a "re-split" that did not
change what the engine believes.

## 4. THE BRANCHES

* **BRANCH (a) — THE CUT LANDS.** G-SPLIT admits `R_s ≤ 10.59` and every other gate passes.
  → The re-split delivers the route threshold; **G-ROUTE's measured N is the headline**, and
  carrier-v2's N = 800 bar is O(N)-reachable. The tail model's gap over the band is quoted
  with the verdict every time, because that is what was bought with it.
* **BRANCH (b) — PARTIAL.** The smallest admissible `R_s` is above 10.59 but below 20.0.
  → The table can be shortened but **not far enough for the N = 800 bar with THIS far model**.
  Report the achievable threshold, the radius that achieved it, and name what a different far
  model would have to do. This is a result, not a failure to get one.
* **BRANCH (c) — REFUTED.** No `R_s < 20.0` is admissible. → **The power-law tail cannot carry
  any of the band**, the near/far split as built cannot buy the route, and the design is
  reported dead with the measured gaps that killed it. The near table is not banked.
* **BRANCH (d) — VOID.** Any of §5 fires.

**Pre-committed follow-up, designed in rather than rescued.** If (b) or (c), the same
instrument re-runs with the far model's exponent fitted over the WHOLE band rather than the
last 10% of knots — a different model, declared as one, gated at the same budget, and reported
as a second model rather than as a correction to the first. No new budget, no re-chosen band.

## 5. VOID CONDITIONS

- **V1 — PLANT DID NOT FIRE.** Any plant whose carrier reads 0.0, or which fires the wrong gate.
- **V2 — THE ARTIFACT IS UNDECLARED.** The near table does not state its form (prefix or
  resampled), so G-EXACT cannot know which comparison it is entitled to.
- **V3 — EMPTY BAND.** G-SPLIT's band contains no sampled radius, or G-EXACT's frames contain
  no pair in `(R_s, r_max]`.
- **V4 — WORK FLOOR.** G-WORK fails on any floor.
- **V5 — THE ROUTE NEVER ENGAGES.** G-ROUTE's bisection finds no N at either radius, so there
  is no threshold to compare and the deliverable does not exist.

## 6. WHAT THIS CANNOT SAY

1. **Nothing about ionic scenes.** Unchanged from B2; the force law still carries no charge.
2. **Nothing about a speedup.** G-ROUTE measures where the route ENGAGES, not what it costs
   once it does. That the cell route is cheaper than the complete one at large N is B2's
   G13 territory and is not re-measured here.
3. **Nothing about the banked curve's correctness.** The near table is cut from it; every
   defect in the original is inherited, including its budget-limited solve.
4. **Nothing above the densities and box shapes G-ROUTE probes**, and the derived expectations
   of 4272 and 736 assume a uniform liquid density in a box whose extent the atoms actually
   span — which M-HOMOG says is exactly what a bounding-box extent may not do.
