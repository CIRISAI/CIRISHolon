## 4. THE dE₄ ARM LEAVES ITS DECLARED PLANE — a finding for another lane, reported at full volume

This is not a rung-1 result and it is the most consequential thing this campaign measured.

**The measurement.** For a `dims == 2` scene every force is in-plane by symmetry, so `z`
should hold its placement value exactly. Across eleven trajectories:

| arm | worst \|z − z₀\| over 20,000 frames | first frame off the plane |
|---|---|---|
| fenced, all 8 seeds | **0.000e0** (bit-exact) | none |
| de4_off `0x…5422` | **0.000e0** (bit-exact) | none |
| hydrogen `0x…5421` | **0.000e0** (bit-exact) | none |
| **de4_on `0x…5422`** | **11.49 bohr** | **frame 4230** |

The onset is a roundoff-scale seed that then grows:

| | |
|---|---|
| first nonzero `vz` | frame **4221**, atom 3, `vz = −1.9e-21` |
| first off-plane position | frame 4234, `\|z − z₀\| = 1.0e-12` |
| frame 6000 | 8e-6 bohr, 10 of 12 atoms off plane |
| frame 10000 | 8.28 bohr, 12 of 12 |
| frame 19999 | 10.44 bohr, saturating against the box |

The banked `de4_on.log` reports `dE4 solves: 0` at frame 4000 and `139` at frame 6000, so
the four-body term first fires in the interval containing frame 4221. **An exactly planar
configuration is being given a nonzero out-of-plane force at roundoff scale, and the
dynamics amplifies it by twenty-one orders until the scene is three-dimensional.**

**Three consequences, and the third is the urgent one.**

1. **`de4_on` is excluded from this campaign's evidence**, because after frame ~4230 its
   scene is not the scene it declares. **The exclusion costs nothing** — it contributed 7
   chart readings, all VOID or NOT CLOSED, and zero candidate structures — and that is said
   so nobody mistakes the exclusion for load-bearing.
2. **It is a second, independent dE₄ defect**, alongside the double mass division
   `CENSUS_RESULTS.md` §0 records. That one broke momentum; this one breaks dimensionality.
3. **It confounds the census's §12/§13 dE₄ adjudication, which has just become scoreable.**
   Arm A finished after §13 was written — `dE4_evals 891`, `|p| 4.87e-12/1.04e-7`, molecules
   `[H2 H2 OH2 O3H2]`, matching arm B's line and satisfying G-dE4-1 and G-dE4-2 as staked.
   But arm A leaves the plane and arm B does not, so **the two arms differ in
   DIMENSIONALITY as well as in the four-body term**, and §12.3's branches cannot be scored
   on this pair as a one-variable comparison. That is precisely the confound §12.1 was
   written to avoid, arriving through a variable nobody was watching.

**This campaign does not diagnose the cause** and does not own `quaternary.rs`. What it
offers is the discriminator: nine conservation-clean trajectories that hold the plane
bit-exactly and one that does not, with the onset frame pinned and the seed magnitude
measured.

## 7. WHAT THIS DOES NOT CLAIM

* **It does not claim the ~10 nm band.** These scenes are 1.83 × 1.10 nm in two dimensions
  with four oxygens. The scale fence was stated before any verdict and no verdict lifts it
  (§7.2 of the freeze; M-VOLUME-SCALE).
* **It does not claim hydrogen bonding is absent from this engine's physics.** It measures
  that the committed Luzar–Chandler criterion, on these ten trajectories, produces almost no
  edges, and that the ANGLE clause is the binding one under two different donor rules. Why
  the ladder as configured does not produce near-linear O–H···O alignment is an open
  question this campaign raises and does not answer.
* **It does not claim the nearest-oxygen donor rule is innocent in general** — only that it
  is not what suppresses the network here. Relaxing it to any oxygen within 2.5 bohr
  multiplies the record count by 1.1× to 3.1× (2017 vs 661; 5377 vs 2687; 134 vs 102), which
  is not the four orders between "distances available in 99% of frames" and "no network".
* **It does not convert anything into a rate.** 0 of 19 structures certified is a count on
  ten trajectories, not a frequency.
* **It does not quote a survival count as evidence.** M-CONJUNCTION-MONOTONE: the passing
  fraction of a nested conjunction is non-increasing for any predicates whatsoever, so "k of
  7 charts survived" would be a theorem about conjunctions. Each chart's reading at each
  gate is given instead.
* **It does not re-verify `T`.** The grain boundary is inherited from the census unchanged;
  its bijectivity is an inherited and named gap (M-NONBIJECTIVE-STEP, M-UNTESTED-GAP).
* **It does not diagnose the dE₄ planarity break.** §4 measures it and hands it over.
