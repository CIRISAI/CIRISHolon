CIRISHolon — live demos

Hydrogen atoms under the exact STO‑3G pair potential, solved by full CI in your browser rather than fetched, integrated symplectically, with every energy and momentum flow on a gated ledger. Two views of one core.

  • The atom world — 2D

    Canvas. About 115 KB of WebAssembly: the browser owns input and pixels, the wasm owns the physics.

  • The atom world — 3D

    Bevy. Orbit the box, drag an atom through the spring, watch the same ledger. Megabytes rather than kilobytes — it brings its own renderer.

Engine and record: github.com/CIRISAI/CIRISHolon.
