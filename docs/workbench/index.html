      THE SCALE LADDER (FSD-W2 §9c)

      —

      A kilometre of water is about 3×10³¹ molecules. Nobody simulates that and this page does not pretend to. What the zoom axis shows instead is the ladder itself: each band either runs its certified physics or wears its fence with an owner and an exit. The rungs certifying one by one IS the story, and a fence is honest content on it.

      —

      Acuity is the allocator. A band's population is what this view can actually distinguish: nothing while the view is far wider than the band's own scale, ONE when the view has zoomed to that scale — the pinned seed — and cubic growth after. That is why a cube of 3×10³¹ molecules is cheap to stand in front of: by the time finer structure is visible at all, almost nothing else at that scale is in view, and everything outside it stays coarse under the de-allocation law. No representative slabs, no bulk fine-simulation.

      The zoom de-allocates; there is no handoff machinery and none is owed. A holon that is not load-bearing releases its members' fine degrees of freedom, and load-bearing is not a heuristic — it is the measured per-row closure defect shown live on the molecular band. A row reading ~0 is autonomous by measurement and its composite carries it exactly on closure boundaries; a row being buffeted, grabbed, or coupled into the visible region keeps its fine allocation. Zooming out is the same accounting event in reverse, and formation and dissolution are accounting-only: energy before equals energy after, bit for bit.

      Two debts this panel owes, shown rather than hidden. First: no threshold separates "~0" from "not ~0" yet, so the panel reports the defect DISTRIBUTION and not a verdict on which rows would release. That number belongs to the grain law's own stated bound and is the engine's to derive, not this page's to guess. Second: this page performs NO de-allocation — none is implemented — so what you see is the criterion being read, never a budget being reclaimed. And the three fences above are not yet rows in FENCES.md, which the fence law requires; they carry their owner and exit here, and their registration is owed.

      THE WATER STORY (WB-9.6)

      RECORD

      This is the only panel on the page whose numbers did not come out of the engine you are watching.       What is settled: water formed and HELD. Two separate blocks of one oxygen and two hydrogens clear a window that was frozen before the instrument measuring it existed — 893.8 fs and 923.9 fs, both CERTIFIED-STRICT, both on moving carriers, both named by the census.

      Both arose with the four-body term absent, and in the second case absent by MEASUREMENT: the solver's own counter reads exactly zero, which is functional proof it never fired where a symbol check could prove nothing, the symbol being inlined away. That run is a true one-variable control — same seed, same commit, same binary, the term switched off — and its provenance is pinned above.

      What is open: whether the exact four-body correction is what CARRIES the system to water. The matched arm — same seed and commit with the term switched ON — is running and unadjudicated. This page does not conclude that the three-body physics is sufficient on its own: that is a ruling the census lane has not made, and stating it even to disclaim it would put the sentence in a reader's head. Nor does it repeat the earlier causal reading either, because the comparison behind it spanned a commit interval containing a refactor and a solver change, which its own author flagged before anyone asked.

      Two qualifications any reading has to carry. A formula census at the final frame is not a closure certification — the rows above are the census's own verdicts. And every certification here rests on an O–O pair curve that is not converged: worst residual 4.81e-6 hartree on the controlled pair of arms against the code's own threshold of 1e-9, and 2.68e-6 on the fenced arm, which was measured at a different commit again.
