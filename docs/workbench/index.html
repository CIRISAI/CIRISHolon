      THE SCALE LADDER (FSD-W3 §11.2) — 1 km TO THE NUCLEUS

      —

      A kilometre of water is about 3×10³¹ molecules. Nobody simulates that and this page does not pretend to. What the zoom axis shows instead is the ladder itself, from the 1 km cube down to the nucleus of a single picked atom: every band is here, and each one either runs its certified physics or carries a DEBT with the build that is paying it off. The fences are this page's work queue and the rungs going live one by one is what the queue emptying looks like.

      Three kinds of warrant, and they do not substitute for each other. The three coarse bands flip on a node-G closure certificate — a coarse view of the dynamics beneath them, certified by the census. The two fine bands hold no certificate and are not entitled to one: there is no coarse view at an atom, there is the solve, so they flip on their EXPORTS being in this artifact, named one by one in each band's debt line. The floor below the nucleus is fenced on physics nobody has yet.

      —

      Acuity is the allocator. A band's population is what this view can actually distinguish: nothing while the view is far wider than the band's own scale, ONE when the view has zoomed to that scale — the pinned seed — and cubic growth after. That is why a cube of 3×10³¹ molecules is cheap to stand in front of: by the time finer structure is visible at all, almost nothing else at that scale is in view, and everything outside it stays coarse under the de-allocation law. No representative slabs, no bulk fine-simulation.

      The zoom de-allocates; there is no handoff machinery and none is owed. A holon that is not load-bearing releases its members' fine degrees of freedom, and load-bearing is not a heuristic — it is the measured per-row closure defect shown live on the molecular band. A row reading ~0 is autonomous by measurement and its composite carries it exactly on closure boundaries; a row being buffeted, grabbed, or coupled into the visible region keeps its fine allocation. Zooming out is the same accounting event in reverse, and formation and dissolution are accounting-only: energy before equals energy after, bit for bit.

      Three debts this panel owes, shown rather than hidden. First: no threshold separates "~0" from "not ~0" yet, so the panel reports the defect DISTRIBUTION and not a verdict on which rows would release. That number belongs to the grain law's own stated bound and is the engine's to derive, not this page's to guess. Second: this page performs NO de-allocation — none is implemented — so what you see is the criterion being read, never a budget being reclaimed. Third: the fenced bands above are not yet rows in FENCES.md, which the fence law requires; they carry their owner and exit here, and their registration is owed.

      What this build does not do (WB-10.6). It does not flip the three coarse bands — that is node G's certification and no page work substitutes for it. It does not draw the interior of a nucleus. And the gauge vacuum below the nucleus is not on this ladder at all: the operator sequenced it after this build, and a band on the page for work nobody has scheduled is a promise wearing a fence's clothes.

      THE WATER STORY (WB-9.6)

      RECORD

      This is the only panel on the page whose numbers did not come out of the engine you are watching. They were measured by the closure census on banked trajectories, and each one carries the artifact and line it came from — checked by this page's own gate, so a citation cannot outlive the number it cites.

      What is settled: water formed and HELD. Two separate blocks of one oxygen and two hydrogens clear a window that was frozen before the instrument measuring it existed — 893.8 fs and 923.9 fs, both CERTIFIED-STRICT, both on moving carriers, both named by the census.

      Both arose with the four-body term absent, and in the second case absent by MEASUREMENT: the solver's own counter reads exactly zero, which is functional proof it never fired where a symbol check could prove nothing, the symbol being inlined away. That run is a true one-variable control — same seed, same commit, same binary, the term switched off — and its provenance is pinned above.

      What was RULED, and the sentence that stood here is gone. This panel said the causal question was open with its control unadjudicated. It has been adjudicated: the claim that three-body physics alone stops at hydroxyl and the four-body correction carries the system to water is FALSE on this seed. Both arms certify the same three atoms, strict, with a control rate of 0.000 against 111 candidates each — §12.3's branch (b), staked before any of the data existed.

      The ruling rests on the CONTROL, not on the comparison. The arm with the four-body term switched off — solver counter reading exactly zero, momentum at 3.84e-5 of bound — certifies water on its own. Nothing in that conclusion needs the treated arm at all, which is what makes it a refutation rather than a disagreement between two runs.

      What is still NOT ruled: what the term DOES. The treated molecule held longer, 2599.8 fs against 923.9 — and that difference may not be credited to the term. The treated run alone leaves its declared 2D plane, reaching 11.49 bohr against a 12.0 half-depth from the frame just after the term first fires, while seventeen other trajectories hold their third coordinate bit-exactly for 20,000 frames. The one-variable design was defeated not by an uncontrolled variable but by the treatment producing one, and a bent triatomic has more room in the space that opened. That question is open; this one is closed.

      Two qualifications any reading has to carry. A formula census at the final frame is not a closure certification — the rows above are the census's own verdicts. And every certification here rests on an O–O pair curve that is not converged: worst residual 4.81e-6 hartree on the controlled pair of arms against the code's own threshold of 1e-9, and 2.68e-6 on the fenced arm, which was measured at a different commit again. Identical between the two arms being compared, so it introduces no second variable there — but it qualifies any absolute claim, and it belongs in the verdict rather than in a log.
