## 1. The program in one paragraph

BOTTOM-UP, from the baryon floor to the classical continuum: a descriptor-holon DAG in which each tier exports a small set of invariants upward and certifies the validity domain under which the tier above may consume them — T0 (nuclei) → T1 (electronic structure/bonding) → T2 (crystal) → T3 (grain with defects) → T4 (rock/continuum) — every export a field on a descriptor holon of exactly the `MaterialBinding` pattern already shipping in `material.rs`, every refusal an explicit `GrainFloor` or `RefinementUnavailable`, never a silently wrong number. TOP-DOWN, from special relativity descending to the Newtonian limit (T5), per MISSION.md's realization order. The corrected floor interface is **mass, charge, EXCLUSION** (held today as the Boolean occupancy of `Core/Lattice.lean`, corrected below to the occupation cap), **and nuclear stability**. The meet-in-the-middle criterion: the relativity track's spin-statistics theorem (Pauli 1940, a strictly relativistic result) must discharge the floor's exclusion axiom — bottom-up assumes what top-down proves. Two binding rules govern everything: **(1) GATE PER TIER** — before any tier's effective description licenses the tier above, it is benchmarked against the strongest available reference simulation or dataset for that tier, matching 99.9% of trajectories 99.9% of the time at ≥2x speed; where the literal reading is physically unachievable at a tier, that failure is stated plainly, the honest equivalent gate (observable/distributional match with stated tolerances) is substituted, and the literal-reading failure is recorded as a MISFIT. **(2) MISFIT PROTOCOL** — anything that does not fit the holon model or the gate as stated is not routed around; it is recorded (what does not fit, why, what it implies for the metaphysics) and cashed as a discovery, in the M10/M11 precedent. This document contains 31 deduplicated misfits (§5) and 17 candidate M-numbers; the literal gate fails at four of six tiers, each for a *different* physical reason, and that pattern is itself the program's first discovery (L1/M12).

## 2. The corrected floor interface

The bottom of the bottom-up track assumes exactly four things. Stated with the corrections the adversarial round forced:

1. **Mass** m(Z, N, isomer) — per-nuclide, from AME2020 (atomic-mass convention, stated and tested; see T0). Mass is deliberately **not** in the additive ledger: the additive ledger carries (A, Z) exactly; mass = additive constituent sum + a strictly negative whole-only correction (binding), twice-nested — the nucleon's own mass is ~99% QCD binding (2m_u+m_d ≈ 9.0 MeV of 938.27 MeV, PDG/FLAG 2024), and nuclear binding removes up to 0.94% more (8.79 MeV/A at ⁶²Ni).
2. **Charge** Z — exact integer, conserved to measured limits (matter neutrality < 1e-21 e, Bressi et al. PRA 83, 052101 (2011)).
3. **EXCLUSION** — held as the axiom already machine-checked in `Core/Lattice.lean`: Boolean occupancy per direction, 64 local states in 53 (N,P) sectors (`sector_count`, `sector_dims`). Credited as-is, with two corrections that the meet depends on (§4):
   - **What is scale-free is the CAP, not the Boolean.** Antisymmetry's observable content is the occupation-number bound n ≤ 1 per mode (Coleman N-representability). Boolean occupancy is exact only in the mean-field (Slater-determinant) chart; correlated nuclear and electronic states carry fractional natural occupations (~30% depletion in nuclear matter, percent-level in silica). The floor axiom is therefore held as `pauli_cap` (an inequality), with Booleanity the mean-field chart of it.
   - **The mode set is tier-relative.** `Lattice.lean` has 6 FHP directions; nuclear exclusion is Boolean over |nljm⟩ with spin-isospin degeneracy g = 4; electronic exclusion over spin-orbitals. The axiom needs a mode-chart parameter (a Lean object, not prose) before any tier may claim the mechanized object as *its* exclusion — otherwise two instantiations of one principle get counted as one witness (the shared-lemma-one-witness trap, already a paid house lesson).
   - **The cap does not carry the exchange sign.** Hard-core bosons also satisfy n ∈ {0,1} with commuting different-site operators. The sign is invisible to every occupancy marginal — a NonFactoring-shaped datum (M17). The interface either strengthens to carry the sign explicitly or scopes itself to the cap and carries the sign as declared whole-only content. Until the meet closes, every bottom-up certificate carries the line: **EXCLUSION: assumed; discharge owed to the top-down track.**
4. **Nuclear stability** — which (Z, N, isomer) exist, from NUBASE2020, with half-lives, decay modes with **branching ratios** (a single `decay_mode` field cannot represent ⁴⁰K's 89.3% β⁻ / 10.7% EC dual branch), and an environment-assumption frame annotation (EC and bound-state-β lifetimes depend on the electronic environment: ⁷Be ±~1% with chemistry, Ohtsuki et al. PRL 93, 112501 (2004); bare ¹⁸⁷Re 33 yr vs 42 Gyr neutral, Bosch et al. PRL 77, 5190 (1996)).

**What the floor does NOT export** (corrections from the adversarial round): composite statistics is *not* an exportable flag of any tier — atom statistics is (A+Z) mod 2 = N mod 2, not A mod 2 (H-1 and ⁷Li are composite bosons, ⁶Li a composite fermion), and the confirmed-wrong flag is replaced by the Ehrenfest–Oppenheimer **composition rule** re-derived at every composition boundary from total fermion count (M18). Mineral magnetism is *not* a nuclear export (electronic, μ_B, 1836× μ_N — exports route by mechanism, never topic word; L19).
