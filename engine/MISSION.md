## Why

Reality does not become a different kind of object when the observer changes grain or
domain. A quark, ball, person, NPC, institution, storyline, agent, and Constitutional Mesh
can each be a whole, each can be a part, and each can be described through the same choices,
relations, history, and warrant.

CIRISHolon exists to preserve that **representational closure** in executable dynamics.
There is one recursive holon, one maximal choice object, and no privileged scale or entity
class. The eleven artifact-local coordinates—Priorities, Rules, Manner, Identity,
Confidence, Facts, Circumstances, Process, Model, Structure, and Premises—plus the
frame-relative Record axis and orthogonal warrant/source coordinate describe how any holon
may vary and remain answerable. CEG/REG+ supplies the operations and conserved gross ledger;
whole-only state preserves what cannot be reconstructed from partial views.

“Quark to storyline” therefore does not assert that a classical contact force governs a
narrative. It asserts that neither physics nor narrative gets to introduce a second ontology.
Mechanical, quantum, agentic, and narrative execution are realizations of the same holon.
Each may require a different update, observation, and certification procedure, but those
procedures operate on common identity and common metaphysical coordinates.

## The computational promise

Most interactions should be decided at gross grain. The engine begins with the coarsest
representation, carries REG+ invariants exactly, measures the unresolved boundary residual,
and materializes finer child holons only where that residual can alter the macroscopic result.
It returns the minimum viable grain that satisfies the declared parity and conservation budget,
or an explicit grain-floor/refinement-unavailable result.

This is not ordinary level of detail. Visual LOD may hide error because the picture still
looks plausible. CIRISHolon must certify that the chosen grain preserves the outcome being
claimed. When it cannot, it must refuse the claim.

## Realization order

The metaphysical/linguistic closure is substrate, not an unfinished precondition. The
implementation sequence is:

1. **Newton:** deterministic classical motion, constraints, contact, conservation, adaptive
   grain, rendered end-to-end evidence, and solution-gated parity against analytic controls
   and Rapier.
2. **Einstein:** special-relativistic spacetime and dynamics first—Lorentz covariance,
   light-cone locality, proper time, relativistic energy-momentum, collision invariants, and
   the Newtonian limit as the load-bearing control. Curved spacetime follows only after the
   flat-spacetime realization is certified.

Quantum, agentic, and narrative realizations remain legitimate charts of the same holon, but
they do not jump this validation order. We are already past the Wittgenstein question of
whether the representation can speak across regimes; the work now is to make each numerical
realization earn its certificate.

## Mission inheritance

CIRISHolon serves CIRIS Meta-Goal M-1: sustainable adaptive coherence under which diverse
sentient beings may pursue their own flourishing in justice and wonder.

- **Autonomy:** no hidden engine may silently coarsen one participant's identity, agency, or
  consequences while refining another's.
- **Justice:** compute is allocated by declared error and interaction boundaries, not by an
  entity's status, product tier, or proximity to a privileged root.
- **Integrity:** a root holon is only root-relative. It obeys the same composition,
  conservation, refinement, and certification rules as every child.
- **Fidelity and transparency:** approximations, residuals, selected grain, and failure modes
  are inspectable outputs, not implementation secrets.
- **Recursive Golden Rule:** no player, NPC, agent, operator, founder, or Constitutional Mesh
  receives a bypass around constraints imposed on another holon.

The Constitutional Mesh is the current root-scale realization, not an absolute maximum. It
may re-root, fork, federate, or become part of a larger holarchy without changing primitive.

## Why AGPL is load-bearing

The engine controls representation: what is explicit, what stays latent, which boundaries are
refined, which errors are tolerated, and which outcomes are called equivalent. A private
network fork can turn that control into an invisible hierarchy even when every public API
looks neutral.

AGPL-3.0-or-later keeps modified network deployments legible under audit. CIRISHolon has no
permissive core, no proprietary dual-license escape hatch, and no undocumented conformance
path. Source, build instructions, certificate formats, parity scenes, and failure cases travel
with the program so affected communities can inspect, reproduce, challenge, and fork the rules
that shaped their representation.

## Structural refusals

CIRISHolon is:

- not a collection of unrelated `Particle`, `Person`, `NPC`, and `Storyline` base classes;
- not a classical force law renamed as universal metaphysics;
- not a renderer whose plausible image substitutes for a valid trajectory;
- not a benchmark that compares throughput after solvers have diverged;
- not an adaptive system that reports only successful refinements and hides grain-floor cases;
- not an authority over CEG meaning, CEWP trust, or CIRISEdge transport;
- not complete merely because a channel bit exists—numerical claims require an executable
  realization and certificate over the common holon.
