Drag an atom into the other one. They will not stick — two isolated atoms approach with their relative energy above the dissociation asymptote, and the dynamics conserve it, so they climb the repulsive wall and come straight back out. To make H2 you have to take energy away: brake one atom with the spring and let go while the pair is bound, add a third atom to carry the surplus off, or switch the thermostat on. Every joule your hand moves is on the ledger below.
