# The Holon Declaration, version 0 (draft)

*A draft of 2026-11-23, in the shape of the CIRIS Internet-Drafts
(`draft-moore-scitt-ciris-ceg`, `-trace`, `-trace-context`). NOT FROZEN, and submitted
nowhere. Reviewed twice, adversarially, on the same day: `standard/REVIEW-2026-11-23.md`
records what those reviews found and what was changed. The register was then extended
from 15 declarations to 60 (every row of `TIERS.md`, the sandbox's tiers, the twin's
layers), with the members that needed; that extension has NOT been reviewed. Section 9 records the first reading of the sessions
that own the CEG and the kernel; nothing in it is agreed. Nothing should pin to this version.*

## Abstract

A holon is a thing that is a whole and a part: it has its own state, and it is read by
what stands on it. This document specifies how one is DECLARED: what it is made of, how its
state comes about, what each reader takes from it and within what bound, in what regime,
what certifies that, and how the declarer knows. A declaration is data. A reference
compiler checks it against closed vocabularies and a fixed set of checks, and answers
holds, pending, or fails with the set of reasons.

It defines no new mathematics. What a certificate MEANS is defined in this repository's
Lean library and doctrine (Section 8). This document says how a claim is written down, and
what may not be written.

## 1. Introduction

A holon is described in several places in this repository, and nothing ties them together:

- formally, as the Lean bundle `Tier` (a view, a step, a coarse update, and the commuting
  square as a field: `lean/CIRISHolon/Tier.lean`);
- in prose, as `OBJECT.md` and the rows of `TIERS.md`;
- as a value in one engine (`engine/crates/holon-sandbox/src/tier.rs`);
- as a drawable rung (`Scene` in `engine/crates/holon-draw-ladder/src/lib.rs`).

A consumer, the CEWP twin, has a fifth: a generated ledger of every claim its page makes
(`docs/evidence/claims.js` in CIRISAI/CEWP, schema `cewp-page-claims/v1`), with a class,
a window, a blocker and a correction per claim.

Each uses its own words for how a thing is known (Section 4.1). None of them says, for
a layer such as the air, what it would take to be more than a picture: the twin's ledger
classes its atmosphere layer as MODEL, "labelled decoration", and its blocker is empty.

The aim is that everything a CIRIS engine shows or computes is a declared holon, the air
included, and that a renderer is one more reader of a holon's view, with its budget in
pixels.

### 1.1. Scope

This document specifies the declaration (Section 3), the closed vocabularies (Section 4),
the checks and the three answers (Section 5), the generated register (Section 6) and the
known answers (Section 7). It does not specify how any engine computes a holon, and it
does not specify a wire encoding (Section 9 proposes one).

### 1.2. Requirements language

MUST, MUST NOT, REQUIRED, SHOULD and MAY are used as in BCP 14 when, and only when, in
capitals.

### 1.3. Terms

- **Holon.** The thing declared.
- **Carrier.** Its state, in words, and its unit where it has a length.
- **Step.** How its state comes about: computed by an engine, generated by rule, given.
- **View.** A reading of its state that something else takes.
- **Consumer.** Whoever takes a view. A budget belongs to a (view, consumer) pair
  (`OBJECT.md`, design rule 2, as amended 2026-11-15).
- **Budget.** What a consumer needs of a view. It is not a claim that the need is met.
- **Certificate.** The claim that a need is met: for one named view and consumer, with a
  bound no looser than that consumer's budget, and evidence.
- **Regime.** Where the declaration is meant to hold. (`TIERS.md` uses "window" for
  something else, the range of budgets over which the carried set does not change. That is
  `budget_window` here.)
- **Status.** How the declarer knows what it declares.
- **Standing.** What the holon is to the ladder.

## 2. The one rule

A declaration says only what the declarer can back, and says which kind of backing it is.
Each check in Section 5 is an instance: a status owes something, a certificate owes
something, and a claim with nothing behind it is refused by name. Section 10 lists what
the checks do NOT establish; it is long.

## 3. The declaration

which the two disagreed, each now a known answer (Section 7).

## 4. The closed vocabularies

`standard/vocabulary.json` is the one copy. The tables here restate it; where they
disagree, the file holds.

### 4.1. Status: how the declarer knows

| status | meaning | owes |
|---|---|---|
| `simulated` | computed by an engine of the declaring repository | a computed step, with an engine |
| `measured` | a result of the declaring repository, read from its record | a source; a certificate whose file evidence carries a sha256 |
| `catalogue` | a value published elsewhere | a source |
| `declared` | an input taken as given | a source |
| `wager` | a chosen position that could be wrong | a dated kill |
| `illustration` | a picture | NO certificate; a draw view with a label |

These six are the draw layer's (`Status` in `engine/crates/holon-draw/src/ladder.rs`),
adopted as they stand. They do NOT replace the other vocabularies in use, and version 0
does not claim to. How the three line up:

| here | draw layer (`ladder.rs`) | `TIERS.md` | the twin's ledger (`cewp-page-claims/v1`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `simulated` | Simulated | (no word: a live computation is not a row) | MODEL, where it is "the output of the simulation" |
| `measured` | Measured | MEASURED (a record cited) | MEASURED |
| `catalogue` | Catalogue | (no word) | MODEL, "an external published estimate" |
| `declared` | Declared | DECLARED | MODEL, "a stated modelling choice"; DERIVED where it is read from source |
| `wager` | Wager | WAGER | FRONTIER comes nearest: "open", naming what is missing |
| `illustration` | Illustration | (no word) | MODEL, "labelled decoration" |
| a certificate's evidence `{ gate }` | (none) | IN THE ENGINE (a gate in CI) | (none) |
| the standing `sector` | (none) | NOT A TIER | (none) |
| QUOTED, not mapped (`row`) | | BANKED, BUILT, GATED, FENCED, CERTIFIED-STRICT, ABSENT, "referee", "MEASURED once", "MEASURED on 1+1D only" | PROJECTED ("Extrapolated from certified lower tiers", always with an interval) |

Two things follow. `TIERS.md`'s IN THE ENGINE is not a status here: it says a CI gate
reproduces a number, which is a kind of EVIDENCE, and a row so marked is `measured`. And
the last row is half a debt. This version still has no vocabulary for a result that is
banked, built or fenced. What it has instead is `row`: a declaration quotes its row's own
status words, and the compiler refuses the quotation if `TIERS.md` does not contain it. So
the words are carried and kept true to the file, and they are not interpreted. A projection
with an interval has no member; the twin's ledger says nothing on its page is PROJECTED,
so nothing declared needs one.

### 4.2. Standing: what it is to the ladder

| standing | meaning | owes |
|---|---|---|
| `tier` | a closed view that a consumer carries | a closure certificate FOR the consumer named in `carried_by` |
| `sector` | a closed view that the consumers it was tested against do not carry | a closure certificate; `tested_against` |
| `rung` | a scale shown or used with no closure claim | nothing more |
| `input` | taken from outside the ladder | nothing more |
| `medium` | something others are seen through or conditioned on | at least one view |
| `recorded` | a row of this repository's own ladder, declared as the record words it | `row`; when `measured`, a record cited with the words it rests on. It is excused the certificate a measured holon otherwise owes: whatever certificate the row has is in the record, and none is checked here |

`tier` and `sector` are the distinction `OBJECT.md` drew on 2026-09-25, as corrected on
2026-11-15: the CONSUMER selects what is carried, and that consumer may be a conservation
law, the tier above, or a renderer's frame. So `carried_by` may name a consumer of any
kind. A sector is "not carried relative to the consumers tested", and says which.

`recorded` exists so that a row can be declared without being promoted. `TIERS.md` calls
six rows tiers; one of them (the fluid element) has a carried set and a budget read by the
engine's gate, and is declared a `tier`. The others are `recorded`, each saying in
`not_claimed` why.

Whatever the standing, a certificate that IS given MUST name what it is for (Section 4.6).

### 4.3. Step

| kind | meaning |
|---|---|
| `computed` | its state is computed by an engine. It may or may not change in time (a ground state is computed and does not move) |
| `static` | it is given as it stands, and nothing computes it |
| `generated` | built by rule from a seed. It has no dynamics: motion shown is animation |
| `measured` | its motion is read from a record |
| `declared` | its motion is given from outside |

### 4.4. Relation to what is below

| relation | meaning | owes | a cross-fade across it |
|---|---|---|---|
| `function` | this holon's reading is a function of the one below | a basis: a file that shows it | is honest |
| `shared_edge` | the two are built to show the same thing in the same place where they meet | a basis: a file that says so | is what the builder intended, and is NOT licensed by the rule below |
| `theory_change` | the one below is a different theory | | is REFUSED |
| `context` | nothing here is derived from the one below | | MUST say, in `fade_owed`, what would make it honest |

`function` and `theory_change` are the rule the scale ladder's code states ("A fade is
honest only when the coarser picture is a function of the finer one"; the CIRISOntology
session's reading, as relayed 2026-11-16). `shared_edge` and `context` are this
document's, to describe what the engine does where that rule is not met: `shared_edge`
names the construction the ladder's code describes ("at each shared edge the two rungs draw
the same thing in the same place"), and a fade across `context` holds only with its
debt written beside it. Neither is the Ontology session's, and neither is checked beyond
the basis file existing. `shared_edge` is a WEAKER claim than `function`: by the quoted
rule a fade across it is not shown honest, and version 0 lets it pass without a written
debt. That is a leniency of this version, not a finding.

### 4.5. Consumers and budgets

A consumer's `kind` is `renderer`, `tier_above` (it MUST name, in `holon`, a declared holon
that stands on this one), `law` (it MUST cite, in `stated`, a file where the law is
written), `person` or `verifier`. One consumer id MUST NOT have two kinds.

A budget is `{ quantity, bound, unit }`, the bound a finite number not below zero. A
renderer's budget MUST be in `px` (position) or `level` (one step of an 8-bit display
channel). A consumer MAY state no budget; the consumer a certificate is FOR must state
one.

### 4.6. Certificates

| kind | meaning |
|---|---|
| `closure` | the commuting square for the named consumer, exactly or within its bound |
| `scene` | for a generated holon: an exact section law, and a transport cost against a frozen reference sample |
| `bound` | a stated bound on the named consumer's error |
| `price` | a held-out increment for the named consumer: the prediction its target loses when this block leaves the dictionary (the removability gate's reading, or the same increment from an earlier search; the evidence says which) |
| `none` | no certificate |

A certificate other than `none` MUST carry:

- `for`: a view and a consumer of this holon;
- `bound`: `{ value, unit }`, in that consumer's budget's unit and no looser than it;
- `basis`: `price` (an a-priori bound) or `realised` (a cost measured on the run cited),
  the distinction of `OBJECT.md`'s correction of 2026-11-15, item 3;
- evidence: `{ path, sha256? }`, a file of the repository; `{ gate }`, the whole message
  of an `ok "..."` line of `engine/ci-gates.sh`; or `{ external }`, which makes the answer pending. A
  `measured` holon's file evidence MUST carry its sha256;
- for `scene`: `parts.section_law` and `parts.transport_cost`, and under the latter
  `reference`, the frozen sample `{ path, sha256, frozen_at }`.

A `price` certificate reads the other way from a bound. Its `bound.value` is the price,
which may be below zero. On a `tier` it MUST exceed the consumer's budget (removing the
block costs more than the consumer allows: carried). On a `sector` it MUST NOT (dropped).
Its basis MUST be `price`, and no other standing may hold one. This is the gate of
`TIERS.md` §0 as corrected on 2026-11-15: a price, with nothing removed from the running
system.

Any `{ path }` reference (a source, a basis, a law's `stated`, a row, file evidence) MAY
carry `quote`. The words MUST then occur in that file, runs of white space counting as one
space. A number cited from a record can so be tied to the record's own text.

A `scene` certificate is for a `generated` holon and no other, and a generated holon holds
no other kind: it "has no fine motion to close over, so `Closed` does not describe it"
(`OBJECT.md`, the scene certificate).

### 4.7. Marks, labels and joins

`draw.marks` are the draw engine's kinds (`INTERFACE.md` §1). `draw.label` is the words a
viewer is shown. An illustration MUST have a draw view. For a rung of the scale ladder the
label MUST be the engine's own label for that status (`label` in the vocabulary), checked
against the engine. For anything else the label is the declarer's and is not checked.

## 5. The checks, and the three answers

The reference compiler is `standard/tools/holonc.py`. It executes nothing a declaration
names and reads no path outside the repository. For each declaration it gives one answer:

- **holds**: every check held and everything cited is held here;
- **pending**: every check held, but it cites evidence or an engine not held here, or it
  stands on a declaration that is pending. Pending is never counted as holds;
- **fails**: with the SET of every failing check's token, sorted.

These three words were `admitted`, `waiting` and `refused` until 2026-11-23. They were
renamed because the Constitution and Kernel sessions use those words for other things:
Valid, Invalid and Waiting are the answers of verifying a STATEMENT, and admitted and
refused are what a substrate does to a ROW. This is a third thing, a check of a
declaration against the files of one repository. Where this document still says the
compiler "refuses" a declaration, it means the answer is `fails`, and nothing about a row.

An answer is a function of the SET of declarations and files held when it was computed. It
is not final: a declaration that holds today fails when one it stands on is corrected.
Nothing is concluded from a date. `stated_at` and `frozen_at` are content; "current" will
have to be decided by which row supersedes which (Section 9), never by the later date.

The answer is a set so that it does not depend on the order in which an implementation
runs its checks. There are two phases, and the phase is the only ordering:

- **Phase A, shape**: `spec_unknown`, `member_missing`, `member_unexpected`, `type_wrong`,
  `id_malformed`, `vocabulary_unregistered`. A declaration that fails phase A is refused
  with phase A's tokens alone, because nothing more can be read from it safely.
- **Phase B, meaning**: every remaining check runs on every well-shaped declaration, and
  all failures are reported together.

The phase B checks, by what they protect:

| what | reasons |
|---|---|
| what it stands on | `below_unresolved`, `below_cycle`, `below_fails`, `relation_unbacked`, `depicts_unresolved`, `id_duplicate` |
| what the status owes | `source_missing`, `source_not_found`, `quote_not_found`, `row_missing`, `kill_missing`, `simulated_not_computed`, `engine_missing`, `certificate_owed`, `illustration_certified`, `illustration_undrawn` |
| views and consumers | `medium_without_view`, `view_without_consumer`, `consumer_conflict`, `consumer_unresolved`, `budget_unit_mismatch` |
| the certificate and what it is for | `certificate_unbound`, `consumer_without_budget`, `certificate_exceeds_budget`, `price_contradicts_standing`, `regime_missing`, `scene_not_generated`, `scene_certificate_incomplete` |
| evidence | `evidence_missing`, `evidence_not_found`, `evidence_digest`, `evidence_undigested`, `gate_not_found` |
| what the standing owes | `tier_uncertified`, `tier_uncarried`, `carried_not_tier`, `sector_untested` |
| how it is drawn | `fade_across_theories`, `fade_unbacked`, `join_disagrees` |
| the engine against the registry | `ladder_disagrees` |

A declaration that stands on a refused one is refused (`below_fails`), and one that
stands on a pending one is pending. Two cases give `below_unresolved` instead, because there is
no one declaration to stand on: the one below failed phase A, or its id is carried by more
than one declaration (each copy is `id_duplicate`, and the id then names nothing, whichever
copy came first). The meaning of each token is in the vocabulary file.

Each reason is also classed, in the vocabulary's `reason_needs`, by what a party must hold
to run its check (the Kernel session's split, CIRISAI/CIRISKernel#27):

| class | a party needs | reasons |
|---|---|---|
| `blob` | the declaration alone | `spec_unknown`, `member_missing`, `member_unexpected`, `type_wrong`, `id_malformed`, `vocabulary_unregistered`, `source_missing`, `kill_missing`, `simulated_not_computed`, `medium_without_view`, `view_without_consumer`, `consumer_conflict`, `consumer_without_budget`, `budget_unit_mismatch`, `certificate_owed`, `certificate_unbound`, `certificate_exceeds_budget`, `illustration_certified`, `illustration_undrawn`, `scene_not_generated`, `scene_certificate_incomplete`, `evidence_missing`, `evidence_undigested`, `regime_missing`, `tier_uncertified`, `tier_uncarried`, `carried_not_tier`, `sector_untested`, `fade_across_theories`, `fade_unbacked`, `price_contradicts_standing` |
| `set` | the declarations it names | `id_duplicate`, `below_unresolved`, `below_cycle`, `below_fails`, `depicts_unresolved`, `consumer_unresolved`, `join_disagrees` |
| `repository` | the declarer's files | `relation_unbacked`, `source_not_found`, `engine_missing`, `evidence_not_found`, `evidence_digest`, `gate_not_found`, `ladder_disagrees`, `quote_not_found`, `row_missing` |

A `repository` check, seen from outside, is the DECLARER'S ATTESTATION and not a verified
fact. A `{ gate }` evidence is the clearest case. It becomes checkable by a third party only
when the file travels by digest; a third party that re-runs it says so in its own name.

`ladder_disagrees` runs the other way from the rest: it checks the ENGINE against the
registry. The engine prints each rung's name, unit, status label, band, cuts and the kinds
of mark it emits (`cargo run -p holon-draw-ladder --example rungs_dump`). A declaration that
names a rung MUST agree in all of them; two declarations MUST NOT claim one rung; and a
rung the engine draws that no declaration carries is itself a refusal.

## 6. The register

`standard/registry/` holds one file per holon. `standard/HOLONS.md` is GENERATED from it
(`holonc.py --write`) and never edited: `holonc.py --check` fails if it differs, and it is
not written while any declaration is refused. It lists each declaration's sha256, so an
edit to a declaration shows in the generated file.

This version declares 60 holons, each of which already exists somewhere in the record:

- **the 13 rungs of the scale ladder.** Name, unit, status label, band, cuts and marks are
  taken from the engine's own dump. The carrier, the step, the relation to the rung below,
  the sources and the parts were written by hand from the code, with the file each
  relation rests on cited as its basis. Two say what they picture (`depicts`), because the
  engine's own source line names the row: the water rung the molecule tier, the nucleus
  rung the nucleus;
- **every row of `TIERS.md`** (19): the six physical tiers, the three sectors, the seven
  rows of the computational dictionary, the two reasoning rows and the cosmological wager;
- **the eight tiers of the sandbox** (`engine/crates/holon-sandbox/src/tier.rs`), as the
  code has them: seven computed, one (the crystal) whose evaluator the code refuses;
- **the CEWP twin's 19 layers**, one per layer claim of its ledger at commit `04e3b4b`:
  the ocean and atmosphere (an illustration), four cards that draw a published table
  (`catalogue`), and fourteen that draw the twin's simulation (`simulated`, and WAITING,
  because the engine is the twin's and not held here);
- **`air`**: the air as a holon, status `catalogue`, with what it owes before more is claimed.

By standing: 1 tier, 3 sectors, 14 recorded rows, 5 inputs, 1 medium, 36 rungs. 46 are
hold and 14 are pending.

**What the tier rows came to.** Of the six rows `TIERS.md` calls physical tiers, ONE is
declared a `tier` here: the fluid element, whose hydrodynamic block the gate prices at
`+0.0390` or more against budgets it drops everything else under, with a window of
`(0.0033, 0.039)`. The nucleus is an `input`, as its row says. The other four are
`recorded`: quoted, with the record's words, and no certificate checked, because their
rows state no budget and bound this format can compare (the molecule's strict certificate
is a horizon that must be at least its requirement; certificates here are at-most). The
three sectors are declared `sector` with the price each was dropped at. The seven
computational rows, the two reasoning rows and the wager are `recorded`.

This is a register of what the record says, checked for quotation. It is not a
re-certification of any row, and a `recorded` row is not a weaker tier: it is a row this
format cannot yet check.

Of the ladder's 12 boundaries, as declared: none is a `function`, 9 are `shared_edge`, and
3 are `context`, each with what is owed written beside it. One comes near a function: the
Local Group's Milky Way is the first 1300 points of the generator the Milky Way rung draws
3800 of, but the rest of that rung is its own, so the rung as a whole is not. No rung
carries a certificate. The relations other than the engine's own dump are a reading of
the code by one author; they are a claim to be checked, not a finding. The same holds for
the three `function` relations among the tier rows (the molecule on chemistry, the fluid
element and the sectors on the molecule): each cites a sentence, and nothing checks the
function.

## 7. Known answers

`standard/vectors/*.json` each hold `{ expect, declarations, ladder? }`. `expect` is
`holds`, `pending`, or the exact set of reason tokens the declarations give between
them. `holonc.py --vectors` runs them and fails if any reason in the vocabulary is
expected by no vector, or if fewer than three vectors carry more than one fault. A second
implementation is conformant to version 0 when it gives the same answers on these
vectors.

The fixtures under `standard/vectors/fixture/` stand in for evidence, a law and an engine.
They are evidence of nothing.

## 8. What the claims mean

The formal content is in the Lean library, not here:

- `Closed`, the commuting square, and the failure as a witness pair
  (`nonfactoring_iff_not_closed`): `lean/CIRISHolon/Object.lean`;
- `Tier`, the bundle that cannot be built without the square: `lean/CIRISHolon/Tier.lean`.
  It is the EXACT kernel. A closure "within a budget" has no single Lean definition yet:
  `Tier.lean` says the budgeted form "adds `Budget` fields when the API meets engine code",
  `Budget.lean` is approximate LOCALITY, and `StatClosure.lean` is the statistical form;
- the scene certificate's two objects: `lean/CIRISHolon/SceneCert.lean`.

Nothing in this document is proved. The compiler checks that a declaration is well formed,
that a certificate names what it is for, and that what is cited exists and hashes as
stated. It does not check that the evidence supports the claim.

## 9. Being governed: carriage as CEG rows (a working assumption; nothing is agreed)

The point of a declaration is to be governed: its claims should be statements the CIRIS
ecosystem can hold someone to, not files in one repository. On 2026-11-23 the question was
put to the sessions that own the CEG and the kernel (CIRISAI/CIRISConstitution#215,
CIRISAI/CIRISKernel#27). What follows is their FIRST READING as relayed, taken here as the
working assumption. It is not a ruling: a new family is Eric Moore's to admit, and nothing
is registered before the Constitution's rc22 cut.

**The shape.** The declaration stays a BLOB: unsigned, and named by the SHA-256 of its
stored bytes (no canonical form, because nothing re-serialises it). Each claim is ONE
`scores` row that cites the blob and each piece of evidence by digest in `evidence_refs`.
All prose (the regime, the carrier, what is not claimed) stays in the blob. The row carries
only this document's ids for the holon, the view and the consumer.

| in this repository | as a row | caution |
|---|---|---|
| "the checks held for this claim over these digests" | a `scores` row; `score` is met or not met, never the bound; the bound and the budget are finite decimals in the payload, the unit a closed token | no float; a row is not signed for a claim whose evidence is `{ external }` |
| a dated CORRECTION (the earlier text was wrong, and stays legible) | `recants` on the old row, and a new `scores` row | never `supersedes`, which replaces without saying it was false. Both are same-signer acts, so the house must be ONE identity that survives a key rotation |
| a revision that is not a correction (a tighter bound from a longer run) | `supersedes` | |
| a tier killed by its own stake | `recants` | a kill found by someone else is that party's own negative `scores` row, which the family must declare it admits |
| a tier no longer maintained | `withdraws` | |
| who answers for a repository's holons | an owner-binding of the signing key | there is no roster of admissible signers: anyone asserts under their own key, and a reader's trust set decides |

**What a row would mean, and no more:** "this signer says the checks of
`ciris/holon-decl/0` held for this claim over these digests". The compiler does not judge
whether the evidence supports the claim (Section 8), and a row inherits that limit. A
second party that re-runs the checks signs its own row; that pair is the witness.

**Names.** A holon's governed name is the pair (signer, id). `id_duplicate` is a rule
within one register, which is one signer's. Across signers a second declaration with the
same id is a different holon; it cannot void this one. An id is never reused.

**The prototype.** `standard/rows/claims.json` is GENERATED by the compiler: one payload
per certificate of a declaration that holds, in the unregistered range `x_private:holon`,
with the digest of the declaration, of each evidence file, of the engine where there is
one, and of the compiler and vocabulary that ran. They are not rows: nothing is encoded or
signed. Today there are few, because few declarations carry a certificate.

**What the kernel does and does not do.** The kernel crate never sees bytes, hashes, files
or a clock, and will not run this compiler. The `set` checks above are the part shaped like
its work (what a set of rows concludes); nothing is planned there.

**Owed before any row is signed:** every `{ path }` source and basis by digest, not only
certificate evidence; a registered family with its polarity, emitter rule, payload rule and
a sample with breaks; one stable signing identity; and the `set` rule stated in a model.

## 10. What is not claimed, and what is weak

- **This is version 0 of a draft.** The member list will change.
- **The compiler does not judge evidence.** A file that exists and hashes as stated
  satisfies it. Nothing checks that the file is about the holon.
- **A gate is a sentence.** Gate evidence is satisfied by the message of any `ok` line of
  the CI script. Nothing ties that gate to the holon, and no CI run is cited.
- **Evidence may be any file of the repository**, tracked by git or not, the fixtures of
  Section 7 included.
- **A `measured` part owes nothing.** Only a measured WHOLE owes a certificate.
- **A tier is not refused when the holon that carries it is.** Refusal follows `below` and
  `depicts` only.
- **A join is checked only where both sides are drawn**, and a kill's date is checked for
  shape only: not that it is a real date, nor that it precedes the evidence.
- **Known answers are per file.** A vector expects the set of tokens its declarations give
  between them, not which declaration gave which.
- **`function` and `shared_edge` are assertions.** A basis file must exist; nothing checks
  that it shows what is said.
- **A budget's bound is not checked for sense.** A budget of a million pixels holds.
  A certificate must meet the budget, so a loose budget buys a loose certificate, visibly.
- **The renderer's budget on the ladder's rungs is a tolerance, not a fidelity.** It is the
  draw allocator's 1 px (`Acuity`, `eps_px`), which holds by construction, and it bounds a
  coarse drawing against a fine one. It says nothing of how far the picture is from the
  holon: the solar system's bodies are drawn many pixels larger than true.
- **Status is per holon, with `parts` and `depicts` as patches.** A rung that mixes
  catalogue stars with an illustrated cloud says so in `parts`; a picture of a measured
  tier says so with `depicts`. A status per view may be the better design.
- **Only the unhosted ladder is checked.** Under a host (the twin) the Earth rung's sun is
  the host's, and its source line changes. That case is not declared.
- **The tier rows were declared after the two reviews, and neither review covered them.**
  The quotations are checked by the compiler; the choice of what to quote, the numbers
  typed into budgets and prices, and every `plain` and `not_claimed` sentence are one
  author's reading of the records.
- **A quotation proves the words are in the file, and nothing more.** Not that they mean
  what the declaration takes them to mean, nor that they are the file's last word: a
  record may correct itself further down.
- **A price certificate checks one inequality between two typed numbers.** That the price
  and the budget are the record's is shown only by the quotation beside them.
- **`remainder`, `drops` and `closed` are free text.** The remainder a tier owes upward
  and "more closed than the object" now have a place to be written; nothing checks them.
- **`recorded` is a standing that excuses.** A measured row under it owes no certificate.
  That is the point of it, and it is also a way to declare anything measured by quoting
  one sentence.
- **Only at-most bounds.** A certificate that must be AT LEAST its requirement (a horizon)
  cannot be written; the molecule's is quoted instead.
- **A twin layer is declared a `rung`** for want of a word; a layer is not a scale.
- **Much of the doctrine has no member here.** Not expressible in version 0: a joint
  consumer of two views, a projection with an interval, the scene certificate's verdict order and the sample's own error as a budget
  term, design rule 1's battery (construction premise, growth ratio, witness-pair hunt),
  the held-out score and the nulls, rule 9 (a refusal names the gate whose passing lifts
  it: `owed` is free text), and preregistration beyond a kill's date.
- **Sources are citations.** An `external` source is taken on trust; nothing pins its
  edition's bytes.
- **Labels are checked only on the ladder.** Elsewhere a label is the declarer's word.

## 11. Prior work, and what was taken from it

- **The CIRIS drafts**: three answers, closed vocabularies with a refusing compiler, named
  refusals, known-answer vectors, "what is not claimed", and the five row types.
- **CSD/5's registry** (`manifests/ui_primitives.json`): a hand-written data file that is
  the standard's one copy, vendored and checked by script (`INTERFACE.md` §9).
- **The CEWP twin's claims ledger** (`cewp-page-claims/v1`): a generated register of what
  a page claims, with a class, a window, a blocker and a correction per claim. The nearest
  thing to this document already in use, and where `owed` and `regime` come from in spirit.
- **3D Tiles**: a tile states its geometric error in metres and the viewer turns it into
  pixels for its own screen. This is the producer's defect against the consumer's budget.
  Version 0 takes half of it (the budget in the consumer's unit); a stated conversion from
  the carrier's unit is owed.
- **UOR Foundation's LexLean and PrismPM, and `afflom/verdant-engine`** (surveyed
  2026-11-23 by reading, not running; MIT or Apache-2.0; ideas only): a register that the
  conformance document is generated from; a gate that fails an "always-pass negative
  oracle"; honesty levels that each owe their own evidence; and a caution. Verdant's
  level-of-detail theorem is a decision procedure over a table of constants its authors
  wrote, and `afflom/prism-ciris` passes "the square commutes" in as `true`. A certificate
  whose only inputs are the declaration's own fields certifies nothing: hence `for`,
  `bound` and evidence that must be a file or a gate.
- **OPM (ISO 19450)**: in-zooming, refining a thing into its parts while it stays one
  thing. The nearest existing modelling notion to whole-and-part. Referenced only.
